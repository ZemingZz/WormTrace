🔢 Worm Counter & Labeler
📖 How to train the counter — tap to show/hide
  1. Upload a clear, top-down plate photo. Worm-packed photos help most — debris-only ones don't.
  2. Tap ✨ Auto pre-fill — it finds the worms and marks them (all as L4).
  3. Zoom in (pinch, or the + button) and clean it up:
    • Tap an unmarked worm to add it · tap a dot again to remove a wrong one.
    • For smaller worms: tap ● L1 or ● L2 (or any stage) in the palette first, then tap them.
    • Use 🧽 Erase mode to clear several wrong dots quickly.
  4. Tap ✓ Confirm & train — this auto-saves a file for this photo and teaches the on-device model.
  5. Load the next photo and repeat. The model gets better each time.
Tips:  • Use a new, different photo every time — re-marking the same one adds nothing (it gets de-duplicated).  • The app reliably tells worm vs. debris, but you assign each worm's stage (L1–L4 / Adult).  • Collect more young stages (L1–L2) — those help most right now.  • Send your saved .json files back to pool everyone's training data.
